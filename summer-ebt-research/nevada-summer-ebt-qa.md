Q: Which programs and statuses are used for streamlined certification or automatic eligibility?
A: Kids can be automatically eligible if their family gets SNAP, TANF, or Medicaid (with income at or below 185% of the poverty line). Kids also qualify automatically if their school says they get free or reduced-price lunch, or if the school says they are homeless, migrant, in foster care, or a runaway. Kids in the Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations (FDPIR) can qualify too. (Source: Summer_Electronic_Benefit_Transfer_SEBT_Program.PDF)
